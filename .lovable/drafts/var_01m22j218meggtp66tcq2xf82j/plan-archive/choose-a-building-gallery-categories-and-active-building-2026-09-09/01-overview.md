# Choose a Building — gallery, categories and active building

The build page's first entry becomes **Choose a Building** instead of the settings panel. Opening it shows a full-width gallery where one building is previewed live in 3D at a time, browsed by category with swipe or arrow buttons. Selecting a building brings the whole package with it — hallways, rooms, doors, frames, windows, locks, backgrounds, lighting, effects — and the settings for the *active* building live inside that same screen, so there is no second settings entry anywhere.

Nothing about the existing building changes. If a person never chooses another one, their current building stays active and its settings are the ones they edit.

## What each person sees

- **Free plan / read-only accounts** — the default building only, exactly as today. No Choose a Building entry, because the existing edit permission already decides this.
- **Paid or otherwise eligible accounts** — Choose a Building on the build page, with categories, live previews, selection, and settings for whichever building is active.
- **Platform owner / Asset Manager** — the same, plus **Save to Building Gallery**: publish the building they built, give it a name and a category, and it becomes available to everyone. Aiming at hundreds of entries, so the gallery lists lazily by category.
- **Anyone who edits a gallery building** — edits land on their own copy, never on the published original. They may then **Share to Community**, which creates a separate community entry other people can browse and use.

## Two separate sources

The official gallery is ours: only the owner and granted Asset/Building Managers can publish into it. The community gallery is theirs: any eligible user can share their own modified version, and using a community building copies it again, so nobody's building is ever overwritten by anyone else.
