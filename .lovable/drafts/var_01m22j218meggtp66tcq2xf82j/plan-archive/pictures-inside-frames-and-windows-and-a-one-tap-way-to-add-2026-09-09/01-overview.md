# Pictures inside frames and windows, and a one-tap way to add MathGPL assets

Two jobs.

## 1. Frames and windows accept a picture, and can be shaped freely

What happens today (confirmed from the running preview):

- Choosing a picture from your device fails silently in the panel. The file is
  being filed under a folder name the storage rules reject, so nothing is saved
  and nothing appears inside the frame.
- The small picture strip in the frame panel only offers a handful of built-in
  wall samples — not your MathGPL asset library, so the picture you want is not
  even listed.
- The Save button at the bottom of Building Settings only lights up for wall,
  floor and ceiling changes. Frame and window changes save the instant you make
  them, so the button stays greyed out and reads "Saved" — which looks broken.

What it will do:

- Put a picture in from your device, or pick one from the MathGPL asset library,
  and it appears inside the frame or window immediately, with the 3D surround
  untouched.
- Remove or replace the picture at any time.
- Move it along the wall, raise or lower it, switch it to the left wall, right
  wall or end wall.
- Resize it, and switch its shape between Portrait, Square and Landscape, with
  free fine-tuning either side of those presets.
- The Save button reflects frame and window work too, so it always tells you the
  truth about whether anything is unsaved.

## 2. Adding to the MathGPL asset library yourself

Inside any asset folder, the first tile becomes an empty card with a plus sign.
Tap it, your own files open, choose one or several images, and they are added to
that folder straight away — available to everyone on the platform.

Only your administrator account and the people you make asset managers see that
tile. Everyone else just sees the assets.
