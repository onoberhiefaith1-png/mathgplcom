# Autonomous System Exploration

Today "Start studying" gives Aura a topic and she works through it in stretches, then reports. She has no memory of her own investigation between runs, no list of what she still does not know, and no way to ask you something without stopping.

This turns it into a **mission**: you name a purpose, she explores your platform to serve that purpose, keeps her own record of what she knows and does not know, asks you questions without waiting on you, and comes back to them later.

## What changes for you

You type a mission, not a topic:

> Understand how lesson notes are created well enough to build good maths lessons across topics.

Then you watch. On the training page you see three things side by side:

- **Live activity** — her sentences and her actions as they happen: "I'm opening the highlighting page", "I generated eight chips, I expected six".
- **Exploration map** — every area she has touched, marked confirmed, uncertain, or not yet looked at. It fills in as she works.
- **Her questions** — anything she could not settle alone. Each one sits there with a reply box. Answer it whenever you like; she does not wait. If you never answer, it stays open and she says so.

You can type to her at any moment — "try the triangle tool", "stop, don't delete anything" — and it lands in her next stretch of reasoning.

At the end she writes findings: what the area can do, what she confirmed herself, what she was told, what is still unresolved. Those still arrive as proposals you approve or correct, exactly as now.

## What makes it self-directed

Every stretch begins with her own record read back to her, and one instruction: pick the next thing that most reduces what you do not know about this mission. So the path is hers — algebra leads her to geometry, geometry raises diagrams, diagrams raise the 3D tools — and anything unrelated to the mission is skipped.
