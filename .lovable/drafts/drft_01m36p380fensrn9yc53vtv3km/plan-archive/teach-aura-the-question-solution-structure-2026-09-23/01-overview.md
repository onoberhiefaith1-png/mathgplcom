# Teach Aura the question → solution structure

What happened in your note: Aura wrote "Example 1: simplify… solved" and "Example 2: … solved" as
plain lines. There was no question underneath the Example heading, no Solution area belonging to it,
and therefore nothing Floating Numbers could ever be cut out of.

The cause is not only wording. When Aura writes lines, she writes them into the **section** only —
she never attaches them to the question itself, and she never marks which line is the *question* and
which lines are the *solution*. So even when she creates a proper Example session, the lines land
loose inside it. That is why highlighting and Floating Numbers had nothing to work with.

Two things get fixed together: the ability (write a question and its solution in the right place,
correctly labelled) and the understanding (why that shape is the only shape that works).
