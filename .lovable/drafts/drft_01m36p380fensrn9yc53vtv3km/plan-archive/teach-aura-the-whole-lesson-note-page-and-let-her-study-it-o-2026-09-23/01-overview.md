# Teach Aura the whole lesson-note page — and let her study it on her own

Two things, in this order.

**First, the foundation: a clean lesson note.** Aura is taught what a finished lesson note
actually looks like, word for word, plus every tool along the top of the page and what each one
is for. The pattern you approved becomes her fixed model:

Introduction, Explanation, then each Example / Classwork / Assignment as its own session —
Question, then Solution, and on that Solution heading the two small links, Floating Number and
Assign, with the bold word Solution above the clean micro-step lines. She is told, in those
words, that Solution is the bold label, and that Floating Number and Assign are the small quiet
links beside it — so she never writes them as headings, never prints words like "Problem:",
"bucket", "chips" or "verified" onto the page, and never stamps "Solution:" on every line.

**Second, self-study.** One button, administrator only. You name a feature — Floating Numbers,
the Smartboard, diagrams, emojis — and Aura goes in and starts working with it, talking as she
goes, live, line by line. Her thinking and each action appear in the Aura panel as they happen
instead of arriving as a finished list at the end. She keeps going without you: picks a topic,
decides she needs a diagram, looks at what diagrams exist, tries one, notices something she
cannot do, says so. You can step in at any moment and correct her, and that correction is what
she writes down. Everything she builds while studying goes into her own clearly labelled
practice notebooks — your real lesson notes are read, studied, never changed. What she concludes
is logged as a finding, and nothing becomes a rule until you approve it on the training page you
already have.
