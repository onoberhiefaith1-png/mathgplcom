# Give Pro and access-code accounts their full building rights

## What is wrong today

Your teacher, school, parent and student accounts are all marked as your own test accounts, which is meant to give them full paid access. The rest of the app already honours that — plan features, limits and AI all treat them as paid. The homepage building is the exception: it decides "free or paid" only by looking for an active paid subscription, and none of these accounts has one. So they fall through to the platform's advertising building, and the Settings gear that leads to Change Background, Edit MathGPL Building and Replace Building never appears.

The same thing happens to anyone you let in with an access code: they have full access everywhere else, but the building still treats them as free.

Two smaller rules add to it: an account viewing a workspace it doesn't own is always read-only (correct, keep), and every student is blocked from the building settings outright — which also blocks your student test account.

## What changes

1. Full-access accounts (your four test accounts, access-code holders, you as owner, co-admins) are treated as paid for the building: their own building, no adverts, and the full Settings menu.
2. Genuinely unpaid accounts keep today's behaviour exactly: platform advertising building, no editing. Nothing about the free experience changes.
3. Students stay read-only as a rule, with one exception: a student who holds full access (your test account, or an access-code holder) may edit the building on their own personal homepage. A school's or teacher's building stays read-only for every student.
4. Visiting somebody else's workspace stays read-only for everyone, as now.

Nothing about the building's look, the 3D scene, the Academy editor, adverts, plans or pricing pages is redesigned. This only corrects who is allowed in.
