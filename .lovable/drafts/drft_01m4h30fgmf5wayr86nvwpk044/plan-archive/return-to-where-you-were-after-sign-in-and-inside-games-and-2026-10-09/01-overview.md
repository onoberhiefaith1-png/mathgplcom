# Return to where you were: after sign-in, and inside Games and Academia

## 1. The 15-minute rule (keep as is)
The current rule already works the way you described: the clock only counts while MathGPL is **not** on screen. If MathGPL stays open, you are never signed out. After 15 minutes away (another app, screen off, laptop asleep, browser closed), you have to sign in again. This stays the same.

## 2. Sign in again → land on the last page
- While you use MathGPL, it keeps a note of the page you are on (for example a class, a lesson note, an Academia Session, a Game).
- When you are signed out (after 15 minutes away, or by pressing Log out), that note is kept on the device and tied to your account.
- When you sign in again, MathGPL opens that page instead of the homepage.
- Safety: it only does this for **the same account**. If someone else signs in on that device, they go to their own home, not your page. Sign-in, sign-up and guest pages are never saved as the "last page".

## 3. Games and Academia (Practice and Play) continue where you stopped
- As a student writes, each line they finish and the line they are on are saved automatically, a moment after each change. If the connection drops, the save waits and finishes later.
- When the student opens the same Game, assignment or Academia activity again, they see their earlier lines, marks, coins and rewards, and continue on the line where they stopped.
- Reset still clears everything, as it does now.
- Assignments keep their timer. The time already used still counts, so leaving does not give extra time.

## Checks before calling it done
- Leave MathGPL hidden for more than 15 minutes, sign in again, and confirm you land on the same page. A different account must land on its own home.
- Solve halfway in a Game, an assignment, and Academia Practice and Play, then close and reopen each one, and confirm the work and the current line come back.
