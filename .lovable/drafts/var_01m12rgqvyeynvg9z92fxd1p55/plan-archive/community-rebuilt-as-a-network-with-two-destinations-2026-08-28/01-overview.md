# Community, rebuilt as a network with two destinations

The rotating building stays exactly as it is — same environment, same gold and blue identity. What changes is what sits behind it. Today Dashboard, Community, MyGPL Live and Settings are four equal buttons, which is why the area feels like a pile of unrelated functions. After this change the building is an entrance with two doors:

- **Dashboard** — my own Community space: my professional profile, my shared material, my activity, my requests. Only I can edit it.
- **Community** — the network: teachers, schools, students, parents, live sessions, shared learning content, posts and search.

Everything else (Live, Lesson Notes, Adventures, Courses, Smart Cards) stops being a top-level door and becomes a category inside one of those two places. Building/Backgrounds/Assets editors leave Community entirely — they belong to the private workspace.

Nothing is created in Community. Content is made in the MyGPL workspace and pushed outward: **create in workspace → share → appears on my Community Dashboard → discoverable in Community**. Unshared material stays private; the existing share/publish flow is reused, not duplicated.
