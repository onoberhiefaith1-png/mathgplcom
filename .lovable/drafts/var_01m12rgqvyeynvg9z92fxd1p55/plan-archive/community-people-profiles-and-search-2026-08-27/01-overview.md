# Community — people, profiles and search

Phase 1 of the Community layer: Community becomes a discovery space for **people**, while the Individual Workspace stays the place where everything is created, owned and edited. The rotating building stays as the Community entry screen; the new Community dashboard sits one click behind it.

## Community dashboard shell

Behind the building entry, a real dashboard in the existing MathGPL dark navy / purple / gold card language:

- Left navigation: Community Home, Lesson Notes, Classes, Adventures, Courses, MathGPL Live, Smart Cards, Community Feed, Search.
- Discovery navigation for the four people groups: Teachers, Schools, Students, Parents.
- Prominent global search bar: "Search teachers, schools, students, parents, lessons, courses, classes and more…".
- Responsive: sidebar collapses to a drawer on tablet and mobile; cards reflow 1 / 2 / 3 columns.

Today's Community section pages (Lesson Notes, Classes, Adventures, Live, assets) are re-parented into this shell with their content and behaviour untouched. No Building editor anywhere inside Community.
