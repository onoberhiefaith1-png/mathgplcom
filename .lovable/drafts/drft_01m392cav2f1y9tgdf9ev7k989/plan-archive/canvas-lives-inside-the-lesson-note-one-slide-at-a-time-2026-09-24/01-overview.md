# Canvas lives inside the lesson note, one slide at a time

Yes — I understand. Today a Canvas session only shows a small list of Canvas names, and the pictures only exist in the editing panel on the right. That is backwards. The Canvas must sit in the lesson note itself, exactly like a diagram sits in the note, showing **one slide at a time** with a Next and Previous control on either side of it.

What changes:

1. **The session takes the Canvas name.** By default the heading says `Canvas`. Once you pick `clo`, the heading becomes `clo`, and `clo` is the name that travels to the Smartboard.
2. **The Canvas appears in the note.** Where the picker chips are now, the chosen Canvas shows its current slide as a picture block on the page — same as a diagram — with a refined chevron on the left and right edges.
3. **Next / Previous walk the slides in place.** Slide 1 shows the rectangle, Next shows the rectangle plus set A, Next adds set B, and so on. Only ever one slide, always in the same region, so it feels hand-drawn step by step.
4. **Size is proportional.** The region you give the Canvas in the editing panel is the share of the note width it takes on the page. Expand it to the full frame and the picture covers the full note width; shrink it and the block shrinks with it.
5. **The expand control is always visible.** The editing panel gets a permanent expand/collapse button (not just the thin drag edge), so you can always push it out to the full frame and pull it back.
6. **The Smartboard behaves the same.** When the lesson reaches the `clo` session, slide 1 appears in that same region and each Next advances one slide — no separate menu, no all-slides-at-once.
