# Smartboard full-screen picture view

Stop fighting the Canvas frame on the Smartboard. Add one **Full screen** button to the Canvas on the Smartboard. Pressing it hides the whole Smartboard and shows only the current picture or video, filling the entire screen.

## What the teacher gets

1. **Full screen button**: a clear button sits on the Canvas picture on the Smartboard, in the top-right corner, and is always visible.
2. **Only the picture**: the Smartboard, the Canvas frame, the edges and the lesson text all disappear. The picture or video fills the screen at its own shape, so nothing gets cut off.
3. **Enlarge and move**: use − 100% + (from 50% to 500%), the mouse wheel or a pinch to zoom. Drag the picture left, right, up or down to see any part of it.
4. **Next and Previous**: arrows on both sides move to the next or previous slide without leaving full screen. Each new slide starts filling the screen.
5. **Close**: an X in the top corner, or the Escape key, takes you straight back to the Smartboard where you were.
6. Videos keep their play controls in full screen.

## What stays the same

- Canvas/Edit is not touched.
- Lesson Notes are not changed.
- Zooming and moving in full screen is only for that moment of teaching. Nothing is saved back to the lesson.
