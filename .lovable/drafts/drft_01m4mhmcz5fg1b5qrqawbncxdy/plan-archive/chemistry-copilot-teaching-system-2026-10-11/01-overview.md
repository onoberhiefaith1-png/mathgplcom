# Chemistry Copilot Teaching System

The same Copilot (no new assistant) learns a Chemistry teaching standard, switched on only when the lesson subject is Chemistry. It works the same way as Physics, so Maths and Physics prompts stay exactly as they are.

## What teachers will get
- Ask "a Chemistry lesson on acids and bases" and get a full, classroom-ready note. Sections fit the topic: lesson info, objectives, prior knowledge, resources, introduction, explanation, equations, worked examples, practical or demo, classwork, conclusion, homework and teacher guidance. Sections are only included when the topic needs them.
- Equations are correctly formatted, for example H₂SO₄, SO₄²⁻, ¹⁴C and (aq)/(s)/(g)/(l). They are balanced and each one moves as a single Floating Number.
- Topic-aware teaching:
  - stoichiometry gets mole ratios and step-by-step calculations with units
  - organic gets structural formulae and conditions
  - electrochemistry gets half-equations
  - practicals get equipment, method, precautions and a safe alternative when needed
- Edits stay targeted. "Replace example 2 with a calculation" or "add five classwork questions" changes only that section, using the existing section editing.
- Nothing saves, publishes or assigns itself. Your current workflow stays the same.

## Checks before a lesson is shown
- **Automatic checks (no AI):** element symbols are real (all 118), formulas are written correctly, atoms balance on both sides, charges balance in ionic and half-equations, and arithmetic and units are right.
- If a check fails, Copilot gets one chance to fix it. If it still fails, the line is clearly flagged instead of being shown as correct.
- An AI review of teaching quality is advisory only. It never counts as proof that the science is correct.

## Six reference lessons
The six lessons from your document: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, Balancing Equations, Mole Concept, Acids/Bases/Neutralisation and Intro to Organic Chemistry. Each is complete and passes the automatic checks. They are kept as separate reference files that can be replaced, and Copilot learns from them as examples without copying their text.
