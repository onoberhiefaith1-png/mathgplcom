## Goal

Turn the existing Asset Library (the one opened from Lesson Notes) into a multi-subject library with subject tabs across the top. Mathematics stays exactly as it is today. Physics gets its real content from the symbol specification you already supplied. Chemistry gets its own library from the new document. No lesson notes are generated in this phase, and nothing is wired into Smartboard, Games or assignments yet.

## What you will see

- A row of subject tabs at the top of the Asset Library: the open subject is shown as the title, and only the other five appear as tabs (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Engineering, Accounting, Economics).
- Mathematics opens by default and looks and works exactly as before (search, favourites, recents, short codes, insert).
- Physics shows its 17 sections from your specification (Mechanics, Energy, Momentum, Gravitation, Matter and fluids, Thermal, Electricity, Circuit symbols, Magnetism, Waves, Light, EM waves, Atomic/nuclear, Relativity, Greek letters, Operators, SI units and prefixes). Each entry shows symbol, meaning, unit and other meanings (e.g. T = tension / period / temperature).
- Chemistry shows its own sections: all 118 elements, formula notation, reaction and state symbols, ions and polyatomic ions, atomic structure, acids/bases/equilibrium, moles, energetics, kinetics, electrochemistry, organic notation, spectroscopy, units. Subscripts, superscripts and charges (H₂O, SO₄²⁻, ¹⁴C) display correctly.
- Laboratory apparatus and GHS hazard pictograms are listed as "Pending" — they need real drawn artwork, so they will not be faked with emoji.
- Engineering, Accounting, Economics open to a clear "Library coming soon" panel.
- Search works inside the open subject only; switching subjects never mixes content.

Several rows in your Chemistry document had blank symbol cells (e.g. Ka, Kw, ΔH, E°). These will be filled with the standard chemistry symbol for that meaning, and marked so you can review them.
