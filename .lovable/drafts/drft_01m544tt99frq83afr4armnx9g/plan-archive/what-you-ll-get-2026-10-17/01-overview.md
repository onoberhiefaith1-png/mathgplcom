## What you'll get

**1. Lesson note subtopic bar (the dark panel you circled)**
- That left panel shows the Topic name at the top, then every subtopic in this lesson note as a list.
- A **+** button is always visible to add another subtopic. It never disappears, even after the first one is added.
- Each subtopic has a remove (bin) button you can see without hovering, so it works on touch screens too. It asks you to confirm first.
- Tap a subtopic and the page swaps to that subtopic's own work. Tap another and it swaps again. Tap back and your earlier work is still there. This works for 10 or more subtopics.
- An **X** at the top of the bar closes it fully. A small "Subtopics" tab stays at the left edge so you can open it again. Close and reopen as often as you like.

**2. Mathemagine light/dark button on Windows and laptops**
- On wide screens, the sun/moon and colour buttons sit in the top-right corner, right under the full-screen button, so you can't reach them. Phones are fine because there the full-screen button sits bottom-right.
- Inside Mathemagine, the header buttons move left to leave room for the full-screen button, so both can be seen and tapped. Phone layout stays as it is.
