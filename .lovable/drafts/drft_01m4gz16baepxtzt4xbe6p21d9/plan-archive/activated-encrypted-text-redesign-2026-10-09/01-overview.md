# Activated encrypted text redesign

The screenshot confirms two separate faults in the Game’s Vault activation:

- The saved expression is painted as plain text, so `\\frac{15}{3}` appears as raw syntax instead of a stacked fraction.
- The current expression sits on a nearly black rectangular panel with heavy bold lettering.

## Result

When a Vault activates, its expression will appear as correctly structured mathematics with a light, premium face and restrained curved depth. It will feel dimensional without becoming a heavy 3D object or hiding the Game behind a dark panel.

This change is limited to the Game’s activated Vault expression. It will not alter Smartboard, Academia, Floating Numbers, matching, rewards, or the stored encrypted expression.
