# Bring Course Edit Suite into MathGPL Courses

Copy the **Course Edit Suite** app (your other workspace project, "Lesson Studio") into MathGPL faithfully — same screens, same workflow, same behaviour — and wire it to your existing Courses page with a two-way link.

## What you will see

1. Open **Skill Builder / Courses** (`/course-builder`). A new **Course Edit** button sits at the top, beside Search and New course.
2. Clicking it opens the **Course Edit Suite** inside MathGPL, exactly as it works in the other project: the studio home, the **Video Engine** workspace (upload, timeline, audio, transcript, paraphrase, language, voice, timing, subtitles, publish) and the **Gallery** of saved lesson videos.
3. The suite's own top bar carries **Courses** as its first link. Clicking it takes you straight back to the Courses page. You can flip between Courses and Course Edit freely without losing work.

Nothing in your current course, class, assignment or lesson-note flow changes. The suite is added alongside them.
