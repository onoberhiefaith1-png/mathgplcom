# One margin decides where writing starts

## What is wrong now

Each writing panel is centred on the screen axis and its width is recalculated from the words inside it. So the panel and the writing keep re-measuring each other, which is what makes the writing tremble and slide toward the rolled part of the scroll. Nothing in the system says "writing on this scroll begins here".

## What you will get

- A small handle at the top of the scroll. Drag it left or right to set where all writing on that scroll begins.
- The guide line itself stays invisible in normal use and in the lesson.
- Every line on that scroll starts from that one margin. No line drifts on its own.
- The left edge of the scroll never moves when you move the margin. Only the writing moves.
- The right edge grows only when the writing genuinely needs more room, and shrinks back when it does not.
- Writing can never sit on the rolled/folded part of the scroll.
- Text size, depth, colours, shadow and effects keep working exactly as they do now; after any change the scroll re-measures once and stays still.
- The margin is saved with the scroll and survives save, reopen, preview, play, test, reload and resizing.
- Each student can nudge the margin for their own comfort, and their own choice is remembered for them only; the teacher's saved margin is never overwritten.
