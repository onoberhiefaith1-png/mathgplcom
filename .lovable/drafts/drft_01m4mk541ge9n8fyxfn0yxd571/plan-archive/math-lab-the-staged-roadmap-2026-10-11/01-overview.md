## Math Lab — the staged roadmap

The teacher app called "Graph" becomes **Math Lab** everywhere teachers see it: the sidebar, the page title and the back link. The web address becomes `/math-lab`, and the old `/graph` link forwards to it. The graph in Lesson Notes and on the Smartboard is not touched until Stage 12, and only with your approval then.

Math Lab is built in **12 stages**, one at a time. Each stage is tested against known answers before the next one starts, and its progress is written down so nothing gets lost. Together they cover all 20 sessions of your master document, plus the other four documents and the picture you shared.

**Already done (Stage 0, needs the rename):** separate app, sidebar entry, 2D plotting, a first 3D graph, the 4D–6D cube view, the first Equation Library, and View Controls.
