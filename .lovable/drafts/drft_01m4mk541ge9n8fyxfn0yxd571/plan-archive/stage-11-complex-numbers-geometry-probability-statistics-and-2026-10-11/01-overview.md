## Stage 11 — Complex numbers, geometry, probability, statistics and numerical methods

Stage 11 adds five new tools to Math Lab, each one opened from a button in the top bar. Everything runs on the device, with no AI and no credits. Lesson Notes, Smartboard and Games stay as they are.

1. **Complex numbers.** An Argand diagram where you add, multiply and divide complex numbers. Multiplying shows the rotation and the scaling. It also shows the nth roots of unity, plus colour and contour pictures of functions such as z², 1/z and eᶻ.
2. **Geometry.** Rotations, reflections, enlargements and translations of a shape. It also draws loci and the conics (circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola) from their definitions, with focus and directrix shown.
3. **Probability.** Binomial, Poisson and normal distributions, with sliders. A live normal curve moves as the mean and standard deviation change, and you can shade P(a < X < b).
4. **Statistics.** Type or paste your data points to get the regression line, its equation, r and R², and the mean, median and standard deviation.
5. **Numerical methods.** Newton–Raphson and bisection, one step at a time. Each step is shown on the graph and in a table, and you're warned when the method fails to converge.

The panel layout matches the other Math Lab tools, with the controls on the right and the drawing on the graph.
