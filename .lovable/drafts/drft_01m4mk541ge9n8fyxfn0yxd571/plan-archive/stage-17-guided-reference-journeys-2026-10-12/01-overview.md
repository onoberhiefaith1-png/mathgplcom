# Stage 17 — Guided Reference Journeys

A journey is a short, ordered lesson inside Math Lab. Each step names a question and opens the right picture: a graph, a solid, a matrix or a concept card. The step then checks the student's answer against Math Lab's own calculation. Nothing is typed in ahead of time.

## What you get
- A **Journeys** button in Math Lab. It opens a list of six starter journeys:
  1. Lines meeting: two equations, then their crossing point (for example, (3,3)).
  2. Parabolas: the roots, the vertex, and moving a/h/k.
  3. Cube to cuboid: a 5 cm cube (125 / 150 / 5√3), then changing a side.
  4. Area under a curve: Riemann rectangles getting closer to the exact integral.
  5. Eigenvectors: directions the matrix does not turn.
  6. Probability: binomial first, then the normal curve.
- Each step shows a question, the picture opens by itself, the student answers, and the step is marked right or wrong, with a hint. Next and Back move between steps.
- A **Change the numbers** option rebuilds the journey with new values, so answers are never memorised.
- Progress is saved on the device for each journey, and it works offline.
- Teachers can include a journey in a shared Math Lab scene. Students then follow it in the student view.

## Why it is better than a worksheet
The picture, the working and the check all come from the same calculation. When a number changes, everything stays consistent.
