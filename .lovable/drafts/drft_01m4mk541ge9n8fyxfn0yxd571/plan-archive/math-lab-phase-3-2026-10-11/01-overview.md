# Math Lab — Phase 3

This phase follows your latest request: **matrices, calculus and saving graphs**, rather than the older roadmap's library-only Stage 3. Math Lab stays directly below Courses in both teacher workspace menus; the Lesson Note asset library and existing Smartboard graphs remain unchanged.

## 1. Matrices
- Add a clearly labelled Matrices tool with editable 2×2 and 3×3 grids, matrix A/B operations, determinant, inverse, transpose and multiplication.
- Show a 2×2 transformation of a unit square alongside its original, and allow plotting the transformed outline on the main graph.
- Reject incompatible dimensions and explain singular matrices without breaking the page.

**Significance:** teachers demonstrate transformations immediately; students connect determinant to area and see scaling, reflection and shear. These ideas underpin graphics, robotics and engineering, making calculated numbers meaningful beyond hand arithmetic.

## 2. Calculus
- Add a Calculus tool for a selected explicit curve, with a symbolic derivative, derivative plot and tangent at a chosen x-coordinate.
- Calculate a definite integral between editable bounds using checked numerical integration, clearly labelled as approximate.
- Handle undefined values, invalid bounds and unsupported expressions explicitly; never present an unreliable result as verified.

**Significance:** teachers compare a function with its rate of change; students connect slope and accumulation to motion, growth and measurement. Immediate comparisons replace repetitive redrawing while preserving mathematical meaning.
