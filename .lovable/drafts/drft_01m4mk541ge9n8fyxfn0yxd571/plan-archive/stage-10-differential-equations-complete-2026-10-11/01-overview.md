# Stage 10 — Differential equations, complete

Stage 7 started this: a slope field, Euler steps and one exact method. Stage 10 finishes the roadmap item inside the existing Differential equations panel in Math Lab. Nothing else changes: Smartboard, Lesson Notes, Game and credits are not touched. Everything runs on the device with no AI and no credits.

## What teachers and students get

1. **Tap to draw a solution.** Tap anywhere on the slope field and that point becomes the starting value, with its solution curve drawn through it. Earlier curves stay faint, so the class sees that the starting value decides which solution happens.
2. **Euler vs Runge–Kutta, with the error.** The step table shows Euler, RK4 and (when known) the exact value, plus an error column for each. Halving the step size shows Euler's error roughly halving.
3. **Equilibria and stability.** For dy/dx = f(y), Math Lab finds where f(y) = 0 and labels each one stable or unstable, and draws them as dashed lines.
4. **Classic models** as one-tap presets, each with sliders for its constants and one line saying what it means in the real world:
   - growth and decay (interest, radioactivity)
   - logistic (population, epidemics)
   - Newton's cooling (cooling coffee)
   - predator–prey (rabbits and foxes)
   - spring (damped oscillation)
   - pendulum
5. **Phase plane for systems.** A new "System" mode for dx/dt = f(x, y), dy/dt = g(x, y): arrow field, tap to draw a trajectory, plus x(t) and y(t) over time. Predator–prey, spring and pendulum open here.

**Why it beats the traditional way:** by hand, a formula exists only for special cases. The lab handles any equation, shows every solution at once and shows how it behaves.
