# Match the lesson-note diagram proportion

The Smartboard currently enlarges geometry twice: its presentation calculation forces at least **420 px**, and its board style then stretches it toward **864 px**. The lesson-note version instead keeps the drawing near its natural cropped width, capped at **520 px**.

## Adjustment

- Use the same natural-width calculation for geometry in lesson notes and on the Smartboard.
- Stop the Smartboard style from stretching a smaller diagram beyond that calculated width.
- Keep proportional scaling, board zoom, click targets, neutral-before-click colours, and the Properties panel unchanged.
- Preserve the existing layout: Properties beside the diagram on desktop/tablet and below it on phones.
