# Teacher workspace: one clean page, credits in the header

The teaching page currently repeats much of the left list and stacks big Plan, Refer & Earn and Credits blocks at the bottom. It becomes five sections in a fixed order, and the credit balance moves up into the top bar.

## What the page shows, top to bottom

1. **Welcome / header** — unchanged.
2. **Overview** — the same four summary tiles.
3. **Upcoming Schedule** — the same sessions and behaviour.
4. **Quick Action** — proper tappable cards, exactly five: Lesson Notes, SmartBoard, Classes, Adventure, Skill Builder. MathGPL Live and Pricing are removed from here.
5. **Recent Activities** — unchanged.
6. **School & Students** — My School and My Students shown first as the main content, with Manage Connections as a smaller action beneath them. This replaces the three separate side blocks.

## What leaves the page

Plan, Refer & Earn and the large Credits block come off the page. Nothing is deleted — Plan, Pricing, Refer & Earn and MathGPL Live all stay in the left list exactly as they are, and every screen still opens.

## Credits in the top bar

A compact chip such as "5 credits" sits beside Search, Community and the workspace switcher. Tapping it opens the existing credit area — balance, Add credits, usage and recent credit activity — so the details are one tap away instead of permanently filling the page. Students do not get the chip, matching the earlier decision to keep credits out of their view.

Locked features keep showing their lock and upgrade prompt, and the visual style stays exactly as it is.
