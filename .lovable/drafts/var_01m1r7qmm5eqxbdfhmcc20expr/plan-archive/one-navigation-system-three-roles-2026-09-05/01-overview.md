# One navigation system, three roles

The Student workspace already works the way you want, so it is the template: a left panel that opens and closes from its icon, and a bar pinned to the bottom of the screen that swaps the main content without leaving the workspace. Teacher and School get exactly that same behaviour, with their own four sections.

## Teacher workspace

The bottom bar shows **Quick Action | Schedule | Activity | My School**, with Quick Action open when the page loads.

- **Quick Action** — the existing five: Lesson Notes, SmartBoard, Classes, Adventure, Skill Builder. Nothing added.
- **Schedule** — today's Upcoming Schedule content, unchanged; only the label is shorter.
- **Activity** — today's Recent Activities content, unchanged.
- **My School** — one section holding My School, My Students, Manage Connections and the invitations that already sit there.

The overview figures stay above the sections, always visible, so the teacher never loses the headline numbers when switching.

## School workspace

The bottom bar shows **Quick Action | Overview | Classes | Reports**, with Quick Action open on load.

- **Quick Action** — the school actions already there (Teachers, Students, Requests, Pricing, Find teachers, School Code & Go Live).
- **Overview** — the existing teacher/student/class/request figures and the school's summary note.
- **Classes** — today's "Classes in this school" list, unchanged.
- **Reports** — the existing School Report information, under the shorter name.

Plan, Credits, Refer & Earn and Pricing are not part of these bars; they stay where they already are in the left panel.

## Shared behaviour

The bar never moves when the page scrolls, never disappears when a section changes, and stays fully visible when the left panel is open — the panel takes its own column beside the content instead of covering it. The Student workspace itself is not touched.
