# Clean Student Dashboard

Turn the student's front page into a calm landing page that answers "what can I learn or do now?" first. Nothing is deleted — the same schools, teachers, classes, progress and Go Live information stay available, just tucked behind a menu icon on the left and an information icon on the right.

## What changes on the page

1. The welcome greeting and its artwork stay exactly as they are.
2. The left navigation list is closed when the page opens. A small menu icon in the header opens it as a slide-in panel; closing it returns the clean page. Every link keeps working.
3. Straight under the welcome comes **Learning**, as four prominent cards in this order: My Classes, Assignments, Adventure, Skill Builder. Each card still opens the page it opens today.
4. Below that comes **Connections** with just two compact rows: My Schools ("View your connected schools") and My Teachers ("View your connected teachers"). Both are shut until clicked, so 1 school or 20 schools makes no difference to the page length.
5. Overall Progress, Class Progress and Go Live move out of the permanent right column into a panel opened by an information icon in the header. When it is closed no empty column is left behind — Learning uses the full width.
6. The Credits card, balance, activity and its buttons disappear from the student page entirely. Teacher and administrator credit screens are untouched.
7. The "Waiting to be opened" notice stays, since it is an action a student must take, and sits inside Connections.
8. Same behaviour on phone, tablet, laptop and desktop: both side panels are overlays, so the page can never grow long because a student has many teachers or many schools.
