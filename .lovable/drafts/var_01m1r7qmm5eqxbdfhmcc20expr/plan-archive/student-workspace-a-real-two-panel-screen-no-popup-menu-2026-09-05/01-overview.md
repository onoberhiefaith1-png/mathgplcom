# Student Workspace: a real two-panel screen, no popup menu

Today the left navigation opens as a floating menu that sits on top of the workspace with a dark, blurred sheet behind it. That sheet is what makes the content on the right look dimmed and unreachable. It gets removed.

## What changes

1. **The navigation becomes a column, not a menu.** When it is open on a laptop or tablet-wide screen, it takes its own fixed-width strip on the left, with a thin dividing line, and the content simply sits beside it.
2. **No dark sheet, no blur, no covering.** Nothing is laid over the right-hand side, so every card, button and line of text stays fully readable and clickable while the navigation is open.
3. **Picking an item only changes the right side.** My Classes, Assignments, Adventure, Courses, My Schools, My Teachers, Requests, Account and Go Live all swap the content on the right while the workspace and the navigation stay exactly where they are.
4. **Closing it widens the workspace.** The strip collapses, the content grows into the space, and the same content is still there when the navigation is opened again.
5. **The bottom bar does not move.** Learning, Connections, Courses and Overall View stay pinned to the bottom of the screen, unaffected by scrolling or by the navigation being open, and the content keeps clear space above them.
6. **Phones keep a slide-in panel** — there is no room for two columns — but with a plain dimmer instead of a blur, and the content underneath stays legible.

Building and MathGPL Community keep opening as their own full experiences, exactly as now.
