# Student Dashboard: four areas, one at a time

Keep the clean page just built and add the small piece that makes it feel like an app: the welcome stays, and under it the dashboard shows exactly one area at a time, chosen from a compact bar of four — Learning, Connections, Courses, Overall View. Learning is chosen automatically when the page opens.

## What the student sees

- **Learning (home)** — the four cards already there: My Classes, Assignments, Adventure, Skill Builder. Unchanged, still opening the same pages.
- **Connections** — My Schools and My Teachers, opened here rather than on the home screen. 1 school or 20 schools never changes the home screen.
- **Courses** — the student's courses, grouped by the class they come from, each opening the course exactly as it opens today.
- **Overall View** — Overall Progress, Class Progress, Waiting to be Opened and Go Live, using the same numbers as now. "Waiting to be Opened" leaves the Connections screen and lives here.

The bar sits below the content, is compact (icon plus short label, with the chosen one highlighted), and switches the content instead of leaving the page. The menu icon at the top left still opens the navigation, and the information icon at the top right now opens Overall View. Credits stay gone from the student side.

Nothing else changes: no route, no data, no progress calculation, and no teacher or administrator screen.
