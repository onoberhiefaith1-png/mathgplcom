# AURA Six-Phase Review — Is It Sufficient?

## Verdict

**Yes — the six phases are the right skeleton, and nothing you asked for needs a brand-new seventh phase.** But three things you explicitly described are NOT yet inside the six phases as written. They are additions to existing phases, not a re-plan:

1. **Tool coverage is too thin (the big one).** Phase 1 built 12 tools — classes, lesson notes, linking a game, navigation. Your vision is "the AI operates everything; the teacher only talks." The six teacher workflows also need tools for: creating quizzes and assessments and assigning them with deadlines, creating 3D games from lesson questions (not just linking existing ones), creating and linking Adventure checkpoints, enrolling students into classes, reading student performance to spot who is struggling, and publishing Smart Cards. Without these, AURA can talk about homework and Adventure but cannot actually do it.
2. **Conversation memory.** Right now each chat turn only sees the last 40 messages of the current session. A Lovable-like experience means AURA remembers yesterday's conversation when you come back, and resumes where you left off. This belongs in Phase 3 (the cockpit).
3. **Usage ceilings.** You asked what happens if a teacher runs AURA 24/7. Phase 6 covers destructive-action confirmations but not daily action/credit ceilings and an inactivity pause. Add them to Phase 6 so no teacher can silently burn unlimited credits.

## What each phase delivers once amended

- **Phase 1 (done)** — the hands: authenticated platform tools.
- **Phase 2 (done)** — the brain: plans, chains tools, self-corrects, greets you with a real workspace snapshot on arrival.
- **Phase 3 (+ memory + tool expansion pass)** — the side-by-side cockpit: type to AURA on the right, watch the left screen change live, persisted chat history, and after this phase AURA can already execute all six workflows end to end in text.
- **Phase 4** — voice: "Aura" wake word, microphone, AURA speaks replies. Browser speech is free and instant; a natural streaming voice is available if the browser voice feels robotic — decided during the phase.
- **Phase 5** — live board teaching: AURA narrates while the sensor highlights and Floating Numbers slide, in sync with her speech. This is the "AI can teach" milestone.
- **Phase 6 (+ ceilings)** — confirmations before deleting classes/students/notes, tablet and mobile slide-over layout, and end-to-end testing of all six workflows.

## The one honest dependency

Tool expansion is the only place where "sufficient" could fail: every workflow AURA cannot do as a tool, it can only talk about. So the expansion pass is scheduled immediately after Phase 3, before voice work begins — text mode must be complete before it is given a voice.
