# Imagine — the fast, 2D mathematics game

## What changes
- Remove **Game II** from teacher, shared-teacher, and student navigation and replace it with **Imagine**.
- Imagine is a separate application from the existing **Game**. The existing 3D Game, its rooms, editor, routes, and student play remain unchanged.
- Imagine opens the games teachers have already created, using the same questions, levels, backgrounds, writing-surface choices, timers, sounds, lives, scores, Vaults, marks, and saved progress.
- Imagine never opens a 3D room or the 3D editor. Creation, editing, previewing, assigning, and playing all stay inside Imagine.

## The experience
Imagine feels like operating the Smartboard:
- a chosen picture or video fills the background;
- one responsive writing surface holds the question and working;
- Floating Numbers remain the only mathematics input and grading source;
- numbered lines, instant grading, marks, line colours, text sizing, margin control, timers, and navigation behave as they do on the established Smartboard/Game flow;
- surfaces use ordinary page movement rather than a camera, track, room, or 3D world.

The active line stays immediately visible and writable on phone, tablet, laptop, and classroom display. Text always fits within its writing surface as the student changes text size.

## Rewards without slowdown
Rewards are related to progress but do not participate in writing or grading. A correct line advances immediately. Its reward is sent to a separate visual queue that can animate, play sound, or finish later without blocking taps, Floating Numbers, the next line, or the timer.

If a device is slow, Imagine reduces or skips visual effects while still awarding the mark, score, life, or Vault result. Gameplay always wins over celebration.
