# Imagine: Game-style writing surfaces that grow, and look realistic

## What you will get
1. **Start, end, and margin line, as in the Game.** Each surface has a fixed start on the left that never moves. The line tag (Q, 1, 2…) sits in a small strip at the start. A straight margin line follows, and writing always begins just after it. The end is set by the text.
2. **Surfaces grow with the writing.** As the student writes, the surface gets wider to the right, up to the board's edge. After that it wraps and gets taller. It shrinks back when text is erased. Bigger or smaller text resizes it the same way. Text never spills outside the surface.
3. **The margin can still be moved.** Imagine uses the Game's existing margin setting, so moving it shifts where writing starts on every line. On a phone it can go almost to the edge of the surface.
4. **Realistic surfaces.** Every surface gets a shape that matches its name, not just a tinted rectangle:
   - **Scroll**: a parchment sheet with a rolled wooden rod at each end. The right rod moves outward as the text grows, so the scroll unrolls from the margin line. Same upright stance as before.
   - **Cloud**: a soft, puffy outline with rounded bumps along the top and bottom, a white-to-pale-blue body, and a gentle shadow underneath.
   - **Wood / door / wooden sign**: wood grain, bevelled edges, corner nails.
   - **Stone wall / stone tablet**: chiselled, uneven edges and a carved inner rim.
   - **Metal plate**: brushed metal with rivets in the corners.
   - **Glass / ice / crystal**: a frosted sheet with an edge highlight and a light shine across it.
   - **Royal paper / plaque**: cream paper with a gold border and corner ornaments.
   - **Leaf frame**: a leafy border along the edges.
   - **Magical aura**: a glowing edge with a soft halo that does not move.
   - **Silk ribbon**: a satin band with folded ends.
   - **Chest / shield**: a banded frame, or a shield-shaped outline with metal trim.
   - **Plain / transparent / none**: stay simple.
5. **Light by design.** Every surface is drawn with simple shapes and the existing Game textures only. That means no 3D, no blur filters, no constant animation, and nothing that slows writing or marking. Anything that would make Imagine heavy is left out.

The original 3D Game is not changed.
