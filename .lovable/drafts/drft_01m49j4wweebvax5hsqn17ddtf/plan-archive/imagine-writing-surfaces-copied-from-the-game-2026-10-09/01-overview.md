# Imagine: writing surfaces copied from the Game

## What you will get
- Every Imagine writing surface will look like its Game version: stone, wood, door, metal, scroll, chest, shield, glass, ice, royal paper, leaf frame, magical aura, cloud, silk ribbon, and all the "new" premium ones (parchment scroll, royal plaque, crystal glass, wooden sign, stone tablet, leaf frame, magical aura, cloud panel, metal plate, silk ribbon).
- Plain, transparent and none stay as they are.
- The surface still grows with the writing. The edges (rolled scroll ends, cloud puffs, nails, rivets, borders) keep their shape and only the middle stretches, so nothing looks squashed.
- Writing, the margin, the line numbers, rewards and the sensor don't change.

## How it stays light
- Each surface becomes one picture, taken once from the Game's own 3D surface. Imagine shows that picture and never loads 3D.
- Each picture is small (about 30–80 KB) and only loads when that surface is used.
- What is lost: light on glass, ice and metal won't shift as you move, and the text stays flat, not carved.

## How the pictures are made
I open the Game's surface comparison page, which already draws each 3D surface on its own. I take a clean picture of each surface with no text on it and store it with the app's other pictures. If a picture doesn't look right (for example, a cloud edge cut off), I retake it before using it.

The original 3D Game is not changed.
