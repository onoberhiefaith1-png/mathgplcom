# Energy Ball centre-launch sequence

Yes — this can be done in **Imagine only** without making play heavy.

## Exact experience
1. The activated Energy Ball leaves its reward position and moves to the centre of the visible screen.
2. It charges and continues its fast orbital movement at the centre.
3. It creates one missile for every eligible reward currently visible.
4. Each missile is a visual copy of the existing Horizontal or Vertical Collector artwork, rotated to face its own target.
5. Each missile travels in a straight centre-to-target path. On impact, that reward activates through its normal behaviour.
6. The Hourglass Timer and Completion Coin receive no missile and remain untouched. Every other eligible visible reward remains valid, including the Vault.

The writing surface, typing, marking, scrolling, and line controls remain usable throughout the sequence.
