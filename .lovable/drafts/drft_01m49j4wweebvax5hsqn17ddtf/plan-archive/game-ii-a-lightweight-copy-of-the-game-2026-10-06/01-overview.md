# Game II — a lightweight copy of the Game

## What you get
- A new **Game II** button on the teacher dashboard menu, placed directly under the existing **Game** button.
- Game II opens its own pages. The existing Game, its rooms, its editor and its play page stay exactly as they are. Nothing in them is changed.
- Game II plays the **same games** you have already built: the same questions, levels, rewards, timer, scoring, writing board and marking. A teacher can open any existing game in either Game or Game II.

## What is different in Game II
- **No 3D rooms and no TV room shell.** The screen is your chosen background picture (or video) with the writing surface floating on top of it, like the Smartboard.
- **Three separate layers**, so a reward can never freeze the writing:
  1. Background (picture or video) at the back.
  2. The writing surface and controls in the middle. This is always ready to write on.
  3. Rewards on top. Stars, stamps and bursts fly over the board and into the score, and they never block a tap or pause the next line.
- **No waiting for a celebration.** When a line is correct, the next line opens straight away while the reward plays above it.
- **Light movement.** Moving between lines is a quick slide, not a heavy camera move.

## Pages
- Game II list (teacher): your games, with Play and Edit.
- Game II play page: the light player.
- Game II edit page: the same settings as the current editor (questions, levels, rewards, background), previewed on the light stage instead of the 3D room.
