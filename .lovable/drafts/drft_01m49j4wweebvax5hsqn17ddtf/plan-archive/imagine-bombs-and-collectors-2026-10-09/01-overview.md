# Imagine bombs and collectors

Upgrade the two bombs and two collectors in **Imagine only**. The original 3D Game remains unchanged.

## Student experience
- **Math Core Bomb** and **Premium Chain Bomb** remain visually distinct, but both read as genuinely round, solid power-ups rather than flat icons.
- Each bomb charges in place, spins rapidly with a spherical/orbital motion, flashes with lightning, then activates the correct visible rewards through their normal behaviours.
- **Horizontal Collector** makes one fast full-width sweep from its nearest side to the opposite side.
- **Vertical Collector** makes one fast full-height sweep from its nearest end to the opposite end.
- Every eligible reward touched by a collector activates exactly when the collector reaches it, then the collector exits and disappears.
- Writing, marking, scrolling, Floating Numbers, sound and line progression remain responsive while effects run.

The feel will borrow Royal Match’s clear power-up language: a rocket clears a complete row or column, while chains continue through other power-ups. It will not copy Royal Match artwork. [Royal Match’s official guide](https://dreamgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-royal-match/faq/6-creating-and-using-the-power-ups/) confirms the row/column behaviour.

## Protected rewards
Completion, Hourglass and Vault remain protected by the existing Game rule. Bombs and collectors cannot activate them. Hidden, consumed, already-active and off-screen rewards are also skipped.
