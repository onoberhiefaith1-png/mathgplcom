# Imagine bomb upgrade

Upgrade **Math Core / Bomb** and **Premium Chain Bomb** in Imagine only. The original 3D Game stays unchanged.

Both bombs will share the same fast west↔east orbit motion, lightning presentation, timing, sound, and deterministic chain rules. Their artwork remains distinct, but their behaviour becomes consistent.

## Student experience
- A bomb activates only when its own line is earned, or when another eligible chain reaches it.
- It rapidly swings and spins horizontally, charges with lightning, then sends visible bolts to the actual eligible rewards currently on screen.
- Each struck reward activates through its normal behaviour; bombs and collectors can continue the chain.
- Writing, Floating Numbers, marking, scrolling and line progression remain available throughout the effect.
- Effects stay in Imagine’s independent, pointer-free DOM/SVG layer, with a short reduced-motion path and a bounded effect queue.

## Protected items
- **Hourglass** and **Vault** are never lightning targets.
- **Completion** remains protected by the existing Game rule: it is earned only from the student’s mathematics, not from a bomb chain.
- Hidden, consumed, already-active and off-screen rewards are not targeted.
