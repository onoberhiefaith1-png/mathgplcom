# Imagine — premium lightweight upgrade

Add every feature previously marked **YES / lightweight**, and keep all four heavy 3D systems out.

## Add to Imagine
1. **Growing writing surfaces** — each surface starts compact, grows with its own text, and keeps every equation inside on phone, tablet, laptop and Smartboard.
2. **All lightweight surface designs** — bring the Game’s available surface styles into Imagine as flat textures and frames, with the correct ink contrast.
3. **Raised or engraved writing** — optional flat text treatments that create depth without 3D geometry.
4. **Writing sensor** — a clear, animated active-writing indicator connected to the current Floating Numbers line.
5. **Premium reward presentation** — reward-specific activation, sound, flash, particles and motion in a separate non-blocking layer. Include the missing ball and calculator treatments.
6. **Music and sound controls** — preserve the saved Game soundtrack and effects, with independent on/off and volume controls.
7. **Premium line-number badges** — keep Question and line numbers always visible, using the selected surface’s visual style.

## Creation and editing
Update both **Create Imagine** and **Imagine settings/edit** so teachers can preview and choose:
- background and opacity;
- surface design;
- surface growth preview;
- flat, raised or engraved writing style;
- text size and colour;
- line numbers;
- writing sensor;
- music, sound effects and volume;
- rewards for the selected line, including a preview of each activation.

The saved choices must appear identically in teacher preview, assigned student play, shared play and guest play.

## Explicitly excluded
Do not add 3D rooms, 3D camera/rail movement, raycasting/pointer picking, or extruded 3D text. The original Game remains unchanged.
