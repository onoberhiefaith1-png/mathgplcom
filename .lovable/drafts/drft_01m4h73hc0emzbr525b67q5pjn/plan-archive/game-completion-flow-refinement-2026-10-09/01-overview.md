# Game completion Flow refinement

The completion screen will reuse the Lesson Note Flow behavior rather than a simplified reaction preview.

- The character’s **Base scene** becomes the default animation and loops continuously while the completion screen is open.
- Tapping an emotion immediately plays that emotion scene once, then returns to the looping Base scene.
- The emotion control becomes its own draggable and resizable strip, positioned initially in the circled lower-left area.
- Creators can move and resize both the character and the emotion strip independently; students can use every emotion button but cannot reposition either item.
- The completion panel, rewards, stars, Continue, and Exit remain unchanged.

This uses the existing shared Flow’s Base and Emotion scenes, so the behavior and labels remain consistent with Lesson Notes.
