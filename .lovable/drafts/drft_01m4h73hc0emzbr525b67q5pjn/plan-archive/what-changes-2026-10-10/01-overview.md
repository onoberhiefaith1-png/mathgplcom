## What changes

**1. Score shows this question only**
The top bar and the completion screen currently add up every question in the game (e.g. "21 / 72"). Both will show only the question being played now (e.g. "7 / 14 · 50%"). It resets when the next question starts.

**2. Evaluation (brain) button: teachers only**
- Teachers and the platform owner see the evaluation button and can open it, in test play and in real play.
- Students and assessor accounts never see it.
- The check is made from the signed-in account's real role, not anything stored on the device.

**3. Each game keeps its own completion Flow**
The completion screen and Flow character belong to the game (for example "HA"). When that game is attached to an Academia activity, its own Flow, character and position appear there too. Another game uses its own.

**4. Academia Play: one play leads to the next**
Every activity has one question. When a Play finishes, that game's completion screen and character appear. Pressing **Continue** opens the next activity's Play in the Session. On the last activity it says **Finish** and returns to the Session.
