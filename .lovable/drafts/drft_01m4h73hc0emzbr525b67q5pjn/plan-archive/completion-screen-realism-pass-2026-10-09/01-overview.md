# Completion screen realism pass

## Goal
Polish the existing Game completion screen so its golden frame and bright panel feel dimensional, captivating, and much closer to the supplied fantasy-game reference.

The approved reward artwork, count-up results, Continue button, and Exit Game button stay unchanged.

## What will change
- Rebuild the board's visual treatment with layered gold trim, beveled edges, parchment depth, corner and side ornaments, realistic highlights, and controlled shadows.
- Make the title banner feel like a physical gold plaque rather than a flat color bar.
- Refine reward-card framing so it belongs to the same crafted board without changing its information or icons.
- Tighten the three-star reveal: star one lights, then star two, then star three in a fast, clearly sequential celebration.
- Add brief glints and restrained ambient sparkle using lightweight styling only, with a still final state for reduced-motion users.
- Preserve the Game background and Flow character layering.
