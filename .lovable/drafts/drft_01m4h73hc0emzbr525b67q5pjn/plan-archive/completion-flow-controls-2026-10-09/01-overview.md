# Completion Flow controls

## Goal
Place the Flow character in front of the completion panel and give it the same direct manipulation and reaction controls used on the Smartboard.

- The character will render above the completion plaque, rewards, and buttons rather than being hidden behind them.
- In the creator's Game Flow preview, tapping the character reveals the familiar move, smaller, percentage, and larger controls. Dragging and resizing updates the Game's saved character placement.
- The creator sees the reaction keys while designing and can test every reaction.
- Students see the same reaction keys on the finished completion screen and can trigger reactions.
- Students cannot drag, resize, or alter the saved placement.
- Lesson Note and Smartboard Flow behavior remains unchanged.

The uploaded annotation identifies the completion panel area the character must be able to overlap from the front.
