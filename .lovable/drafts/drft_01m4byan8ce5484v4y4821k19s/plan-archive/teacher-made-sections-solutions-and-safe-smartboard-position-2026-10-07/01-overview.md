# Teacher-made Sections, Solutions and safe Smartboard positions

Teachers decide the lesson's structure themselves. The Smartboard follows that structure exactly. Separately, the Smartboard keeps notes, floating numbers and the writing caret below the Solution. Floating Numbers, the Solution package and the Smartboard design stay the same.

## 1. No fixed starting sections
- New lesson notes no longer start with a ready-made Introduction / Example / Explanation / Classwork layout.
- Those words are just names a teacher can use. Any heading made with SECTION counts as a real Section, whatever its text ("Revision", "Example 2", "Warm-up"...).
- Notes you already have keep their headings, and those headings stay Sections.

## 2. Two quick buttons at the bottom of the editor
- **SECTION** is an on/off switch. When it is on it glows, and whatever you type becomes a large Section heading. Press it again to go back to normal writing.
- **SOLUTION** is a single press. It puts the existing Solution package exactly where your cursor is, with its AI, assign, caret and floating-number features unchanged. It never moves itself to the top or bottom.

## 3. A Solution belongs to its Section
- A Solution is part of the nearest Section above it, never a Section of its own.
- Solutions are numbered inside their Section, for example Example 1 → Solution 1 and Classwork 1 → Solution for Classwork 1.
- The section tabs at the top of the editor list only Sections.
